User Agreement

Terms of Service

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Welcome to Chitthi ("Platform", "we", "our"). By accessing our website, creating drafts, subscribing to themed clubs, or purchasing delivery credits, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms of Service.

1. Scope of Service

Chitthi is a hybrid technology and stationery platform operated by Team Manka Kura in Nepal. We provide online letter composing tools, future delivery scheduling systems, and physical printing, packaging, and shipping coordination across Nepal boundaries.

2. Delivery & Scheduling Guarantee

For standard letters, we aim to deliver the envelope within the first week of purchase. For scheduled "Future Letters", we guarantee secure vault archiving of the digital draft and commit to physical doorstep dispatch in the weeks leading up to the designated date (up to a 5-year maximum). While we partner with reputable courier networks to ensure timely arrival, we are not responsible for delays caused by natural disasters, roadblocks, or inaccurate address fields provided by the sender.

3. Word Limit Restrictors & Calligraphy Surcharges

Standard letters are limited to 500 words. Typing beyond this limit requires checking the "Expanded words" option in the editor, adding an automatic NPR 20 fee, up to a maximum limit of 1000 words. If the handwritten calligraphy service is checked, a fee of NPR 20 per 500-word block is applied (totaling NPR 20 or NPR 40 depending on your final word length).

4. 10-Day Local Draft Policy

To support user privacy and data optimization, unfinished drafts are stored locally inside the user's browser (localStorage) for a maximum of **10 days** from creation. If the user does not submit or finalize the letter within this timeframe, the local cache will expire and clear. It is the user's responsibility to complete the draft before this threshold.

5. Subscriptions & Payments

Monthly Snail Mail clubs (Healing, Fan, Vintage, Horoscope) require manual renewal every month. Subscribing to the Annual Mystery Snail Mail (NPR 2000) is a single, non-refundable lump-sum transaction. It requires active user registration and login. Annual subscribers receive curated letters where the content and styling are entirely managed by Chitthi.

6. Prohibited Content

Users are strictly prohibited from writing letters that contain threatening, defamatory, harassing, or illegal content. We reserve the right to flag and cancel any order that violates local laws of Nepal, without issuing a refund.
