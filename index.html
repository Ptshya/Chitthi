A Promise Kept

"A letter is a promise you keep to your future."

Chitthi began as a forgotten letter inside an old book in Bhaktapur—written by a grandmother on the day her granddaughter was born, and found years later after she had passed. We built Chitthi so that everyone in Nepal could preserve words that travel through time.
