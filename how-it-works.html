The Process

How Chitthi Works

We bridge digital convenience with tactile physical experiences. Here is a transparent look at how your letter transforms from words on a screen into a beautiful keepsake in a mailbox.

✦ Step 01

Write and Customize

Open the Writing Desk from your browser. Draft your message, add your recipient's name, and choose your delivery model. You can send a Standard Letter immediately, schedule a Future Letter capsule up to five years out, or select one of our curated monthly Clubs.

We automatically save your drafts locally in your browser's workspace for up to 10 days, allowing you to edit or complete them whenever inspiration strikes.

✦ Step 02

Material Curation & Printing

We print your words on warm, high-grade cardstock. To ensure the experience feels incredibly premium, Chitthi curates all materials.

Depending on your chosen themed club or single selection, we tuck custom bookmarks, zodiac decals, or illustration prints inside the package. Vintage orders are finished with a real hot carmine wax seal hand-pressed in our Kathmandu workshop.

✦ Step 03

Confidentiality & Safe Storage

Letters written for future delivery are encrypted in our digital vault. Backup nodes guarantee that your letter cannot be lost over time.

To give letter the feel of actual aged letter, physical envelopes are printed and compiled when they are ordered. So the letters sit safely in warehouse storage for years, giving them the feel of authentic timeless keepsakes.

✦ Step 04

Doorstep Delivery

We coordinate with Nepal's postal services to deliver packages directly to the recipient's home or office.

Delivery is 100% free throughout the federal boundaries of Nepal. Single standard letters arrive within the first week of writing, themed monthly clubs arrive during the first week of the next month, and future letters land precisely on their targeted anniversary dates.
