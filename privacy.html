Privacy & Encryption

Last updated: July 18, 2026

At Chitthi, we understand that writing a letter is a deeply personal and vulnerable act. We treat your messages, recipient addresses, and contact parameters with the highest degree of respect, confidentiality, and security.

1. Zero-Read Policy

Our team operates under a strict **Zero-Read Policy** for standard and scheduled letters. The printing process is automated and handled under strict confidentiality protocols. Senders can rest assured that their personal words are not parsed, reviewed, or shared with third parties.

2. Database Encryption Standards

All digital letter bodies submitted through our checkout wizard are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards before being stored in our primary database. Secondary backup nodes are also encrypted to guarantee that data cannot be intercepted or leaked. Your messages are decrypted only at the printing hub near the target release window.

3. Local Browser Storage (Cookies & Cache)

We use local storage (`localStorage`) in your browser to save active drafts. This draft data expires and is permanently cleared after **10 days** from the timestamp of creation. We do not use trackers, analytical cookies, or adware scripts.

4. Transactional Security

Chitthi does not collect or store your payment details. All financial transactions are completed via integrated eSewa and Khalti APIs. Your session credentials and wallet keys are securely processed directly on their encrypted servers.

5. Data Deletion Requests

If you wish to cancel a future scheduled letter and request the permanent deletion of your encrypted text from our digital vault prior to the delivery window, please contact our support team at npchitthi@gmail.com with your transaction ID.
