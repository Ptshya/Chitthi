Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't people just use WhatsApp or Messenger?

Digital messages are instant, cheap, and easily buried under hundreds of daily notifications. Nobody scrolls back five years to read old birthday texts. Chitthi creates a physical, beautifully packaged keepsake that can be scheduled for future delivery, making the message far more memorable, rare, and emotionally impactful.

How will you store letters securely?

All digital letter submissions are stored in an encrypted database with secondary backup nodes. To minimize warehouse risks and maintain absolute confidentiality, we only print and assemble the physical envelopes near the scheduled release date. Your contents remain private and secure until we hand-stamp the seal.

Why would customers pay for letters?

People in Nepal already spend thousands of Rupees on chocolates, roses, and generic mugs that are easily forgotten. Chitthi offers a highly personalized, luxury emotional gifting experience at approachable rates (NPR 100–250). It transforms a heartfelt note into a lifelong memory.

What makes Chitthi different from a normal courier service?

A courier service only transports pre-made packages from point A to point B. Chitthi provides the entire end-to-end emotional writing experience. We host the typing interface, manage long-term scheduling (up to 5 years), curate the cards, bookmarks, and stickers, hand-write/print the sheets, seal them, and execute the doorstep delivery.

Is this service scalable across Nepal?

Yes. While our core printing and hot wax styling hub is located in the Kathmandu Valley, we partner with reliable national courier networks and district post offices to deliver envelopes to homes, universities, and offices across all 7 provinces of Nepal, 100% free of shipping fees.

What payment models do you support?

We support major local digital wallets in Nepal—specifically **eSewa** and **Khalti**. When you finalize your letter draft and address form, you can instantly complete the transaction via these gateways.

What is the draft expiry clock?

To ensure our system runs efficiently, unfinished letters are stored in your local browser storage for **10 days**. You can restore and edit your draft at any point within this timeframe. After 10 days, the draft is cleared to protect your privacy.
