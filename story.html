Our Story

A Promise Kept

"A letter is a promise you keep to your future."

A letter is a promise you keep to your future.

Chitthi began as a letter that took ten years to arrive.

A grandmother in Bhaktapur wrote to her granddaughter on the morning of her birth, a letter meant to be opened on the day she got married. Life took its turns. The letter was tucked inside a book, forgotten, then quietly rediscovered on a rainy afternoon, years after she was gone.

That letter did more than any message ever could. It travelled through time.

We built Chitthi so that everyone in Nepal could send letters like that one, letters that arrive not when it's convenient, but when it matters most. On birthdays. Anniversaries. Graduations. On the days we wish we had someone's voice, and instead, we have their words.

— the Chitthi team ✦
