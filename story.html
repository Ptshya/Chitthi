Our Narrative

A Promise Kept

A letter is a promise you keep to your future.

Chitthi began as a letter that took ten years to arrive.

A grandmother in Bhaktapur wrote to her granddaughter on the morning of her birth — a letter meant to be opened on the day she got married. Life took its turns. The letter was tucked inside a book, forgotten, then quietly rediscovered on a rainy afternoon, years after she was gone.

That letter did more than any message ever could. It travelled through time.

We built Chitthi so that everyone in Nepal could send letters like that one — letters that arrive not when it's convenient, but when it matters most. On birthdays. Anniversaries. Graduations. On the days we wish we had someone's voice, and instead, we have their words.

— the Chitthi team ✦

Our Core Beliefs

Tactile Over Digital

In an era where thousands of notifications are sent daily on WhatsApp and Messenger, special words get buried instantly. We believe physical ink and textured paper carry weight, intention, and longevity that a screen can never replicate.

Slow living & Aesthetics

Inspired by journaling, scraping, and vintage craftsmanship, our packaging is treated like a work of art. By combining quality cardstock, keychains, themed stamps, and wax seals, we turn simple correspondence into a luxury gifting experience.

Total Delivery Trust

Whether a letter is destined for next week or five years from now, our commitment is absolute. We employ secure backups for digital scheduling and maintain a structured packaging workflow to deliver your words exactly on the target anniversary.
